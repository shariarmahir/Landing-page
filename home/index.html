The portfolio

Two clear needs.
One disciplined platform.

The portfolio pairs daily-care convenience for pet parents with guided access to international education. Each company is differentiated by a human service layer alongside a practical digital journey.

01 / BILUIBABA

Pet care, in one place.

Premium products, vet connections, adoption listings and secure delivery for pet parents seeking reliable everyday support.

02 / STUDY-INSIGHTS

Global study, made clearer.

Transparent support from university selection through visa processing and pre-departure preparation for ambitious students.

03 / SHARED PLATFORM

People before transactions.

Specialist teams, local relationships and a consistent operating base in Dhaka turn important life decisions into trusted experiences.
