Company profiles

High-trust services for important decisions.

Two companies addressing distinct customer journeys - pet parenthood and international education - with human guidance, reliable delivery and a digital-first mindset.

01 / Biluibaba

Bangladesh's one-stop pet service platform.

Biluibaba brings together premium food, accessories and grooming items with online vet appointments, expert health advice, verified adoption listings and dependable pet transport. The intention is simple: make responsible pet care easier, smarter and more loving.

01

E-commerce

Food, toys, grooming supplies and accessories in one convenient shop.

02

Vet service

Online consultations, appointments and home visits with experienced vets.

03

Pet delivery

Secure, trained-handler transport built around comfort and timely delivery.

04

Pet adoption

Responsible adoption listings that help pets find caring homes.

Biluibaba vision

A compassionate digital ecosystem.

Biluibaba's stated vision is to become a trusted, comprehensive ecosystem for pet owners and animal lovers, connecting people to quality care, a loving home and a better life for every pet.

02 / Study-Insights

The honest route to a global academic journey.

Founded in March 2026 in Dhaka, Study-Insights provides transparent, expert support for students who aspire to study in Europe, the UK and Australia. The company simplifies a complex journey without losing sight of the student's best fit.

01

University selection

Expert guidance to identify the right institution and programme.

02

Application support

End-to-end help with documents and submissions.

03

04

05

Career counselling

Advice on pathways, professional direction and job markets.

06

Orientation and preparation for life abroad.
