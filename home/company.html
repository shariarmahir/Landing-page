About Us

Who We Are

Welcome to Study Insights — an education consultancy firm dedicated to bridging the gap between ambitious students and world-class international education.

Founded in March 2026, we were established with a singular mission: to provide honest, transparent, and expert guidance to students aspiring to study in Europe, the UK, and Australia.

At Study Insights, we believe that education is the most powerful tool for shaping a bright future. We understand that the path to international study can be complex, and we take great pleasure in simplifying that journey — ensuring every student we work with finds the future they truly deserve.

Study Destinations

Where We Send Students

We guide students to top universities across 8 destination countries spanning the UK, Europe, and Australia.

United Kingdom
London · Manchester · Edinburgh

World-renowned universities including Oxford, Cambridge, and Imperial College. Strong post-study work visa options.

Germany
Berlin · Munich · Hamburg

Tuition-free public universities, strong engineering and tech programs, and Europe's largest economy.

Netherlands
Amsterdam · Rotterdam · Utrecht

Highest number of English-taught programs in non-English Europe. Innovation-driven academic culture.

Sweden
Stockholm · Gothenburg · Lund

Innovation leader in sustainability and technology. Top-ranked universities with generous scholarship programs.

Norway
Oslo · Bergen · Trondheim

Free tuition at public universities for all nationalities. Outstanding quality of life and research funding.

Australia
Sydney · Melbourne · Brisbane

Group of Eight research universities, excellent post-study work rights, and a welcoming multicultural environment.

Finland
Helsinki · Tampere · Turku

World-leading education system with affordable tuition, strong tech ecosystem, and high quality of life.

Denmark
Copenhagen · Aarhus · Odense

Problem-based learning approach, strong design and sustainability focus, and excellent English proficiency nationwide.
