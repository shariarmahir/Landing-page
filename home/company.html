01 — Company profile

We map the motion of money.

Founded in 2022 by Mahir Shariar Mahin, ORRERY unifies ledger data, revenue recognition and forecasting into a single operating picture for finance teams at mid-market companies. Instead of stitching spreadsheets to three tools, our customers close their books in days, not weeks — and see where the business is heading while they can still steer it.

The mid-market spends more than $8B a year on finance tooling built for enterprises — and spreadsheets for everyone else. We build for the 200,000 companies in between: finance teams of ten to fifty people, growing thirty percent a year, drowning in reconciliation. They do not need another dashboard. They need the books to close themselves.
