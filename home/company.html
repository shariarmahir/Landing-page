About Us

Who We Are

Welcome to Study Insights — an education consultancy firm dedicated to bridging the gap between ambitious students and world-class international education.

Founded in March 2026, we were established with a singular mission: to provide honest, transparent, and expert guidance to students aspiring to study in Europe, the UK, and Australia.

At Study Insights, we believe that education is the most powerful tool for shaping a bright future. We understand that the path to international study can be complex, and we take great pleasure in simplifying that journey — ensuring every student we work with finds the future they truly deserve.
